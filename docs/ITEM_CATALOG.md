## Stat meanings

- Attack, vitality and defense are flat base values before refinement.
- Critical chance and cultivation rate are percentage-point bonuses. A value of 2 means +2%.
- HP restored and Qi granted are one-use potion effects. Qi is capped at the current bottleneck.
- Material grade is a catalog classification, not a combat bonus.
- Trial access is the number of tribulations opened by one pill. The Foundation Pill follows the existing trial rules.
- This list adds no new drop, equip or potion-use rules. The active Cloudveil Sword, Jade Dew, Clear Insight, Foundation Pill and Cloud Iron retain their existing behavior.

The machine-readable source is `src/game/item-catalog.json`. The spreadsheet-friendly export is `public/data/item-catalog.csv`. The in-game catalog is available from Inventory.
